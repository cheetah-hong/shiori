Shiori Privacy Policy

Shiori is an iPhone app that makes a Japanese word book from photos of your textbook. The Korean version of this policy is the original.

Information we collect

Shiori collects no personal information. The app does not connect to the internet and uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Information kept on the device

The words you add, your playlists, study records and settings are stored only in the app's storage on this device.

A log of errors in the app (time, error text, code location) also stays on the device. It leaves the device only if you copy it yourself from the About screen.

Deleting the app deletes this information from the device as well. For the copy in a device backup, see Device backups below.

Photos and camera

The camera and the photo library are used only to take or choose a textbook page and add its words.

The text in a photo is read on the device, and the copy of the photo the app made is deleted after reading. Photos are not stored or sent anywhere.

Device backups

iCloud and computer backups of the iPhone include your words and study records, so they come back when you move to a new device or restore. Backups are handled by Apple's device backup, and the developer cannot see them.

The copy in a backup is deleted when that backup is deleted.

The error log and the dictionary and pronunciation files the app can rebuild are left out of backups.

Sharing with third parties

No personal information is shared with, or processed by, third parties.

Children

No personal information is collected from anyone, including children under 14.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the change is announced with an app update in the app's privacy policy screen and on this page.
